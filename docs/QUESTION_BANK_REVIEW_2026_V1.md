# Question bank review — 2026 v1

Review date: 31 July 2026  
Scope: the 150 MCQs identified by the explanation prefix `[Original bank 2026 v1]`

## Decision

- **50 questions approved as `reviewed`**: one canonical question for each distinct knowledge check.
- **100 questions retained as `draft`**: two presentation variants per knowledge check use the same correct fact and distractors and are therefore near-duplicates, not distinct exam questions.
- **0 questions published**: publication remains a separate admin decision after mock-paper balancing and final proofreading.

## Checks completed

- every approved question is mapped to a 2026–2028 syllabus objective and its parent topic
- every approved question is a one-mark MCQ with exactly four options
- every approved answer index is within the valid option range
- the keyed option agrees with the stored one-mark mark scheme and explanation
- stems are self-contained and do not depend on an unavailable image, table, or scenario
- terminology and correct facts were checked for the intended Cambridge O Level Computer Science level
- exact conceptual duplication was measured across the complete generated bank

## Coverage after review

The approved set contains 25 unique questions for Paper 1 topics and 25 unique questions for Paper 2 topics. This is not enough for either 75-mark mock. The existing draft mocks must remain unpublished until 50 additional distinct, reviewed questions have been written for each paper.

## Required rewrite work

Replace the 100 draft variants with genuinely different assessment tasks. Suitable replacements should include calculations, trace tables, short scenarios, pseudocode, SQL, logic diagrams, and structured-response items rather than simple rewordings of the approved fact recall questions.
